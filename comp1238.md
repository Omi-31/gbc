## Week 4 - Keyboards And Editors
- There are several keyboard layouts (QWERTY, AZERTY, Dvorak).

## Week 5 - Command Line Interfaces
- CLIs are very resource efficient compared to graphical interfaces.
- ClIs are better at automating tasks through scripting.
- CLIs have short command lengths due to historical reasons.
